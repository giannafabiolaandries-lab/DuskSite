Our Story

Dusk till Dango started with a simple idea during Theme Week. We wanted to make something soft, sweet, and cute that feels like the end of a good day.

Dusk is our favorite time. It is warm, golden, a little dreamy, and perfect for sharing sweets with the people you love. Dango is our way of sharing that feeling.

We are not a big shop. We are five friends making, tasting, laughing, and learning together. Every idea in this project comes from us.
